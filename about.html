A specialist consultancy for financial services

Emblaris Inc. is a Canadian consulting company, established in Toronto in 2025, providing professional services to clients across North America, primarily within the financial services industry.

Who we are

We specialize in software and business transformation, including operating model design, enterprise planning, implementation advisory and transformation strategy.

Our approach is grounded in a strong commitment to client outcomes and a genuine understanding of each client's business, operational and technology environment. We are passionate about helping financial institutions optimize their investment environments by improving processes, strengthening technology solutions and aligning operating models with evolving business needs.

Emblaris combines practical industry experience with a collaborative delivery approach to develop sustainable, holistic solutions, aligned with industry best practice and your long-term business objectives.

What we stand for

The principles that shape every engagement.

Outcomes first

We measure our work by the results our clients achieve, not by the hours we bill or the documents we produce.

MK

Mikaela Karlsson

Founder and Lead Solutions Architect

Mikaela leads every Emblaris engagement and is responsible for delivery, client coordination and the overall quality of our work.

She has extensive experience across financial services, financial technology, buy-side operations, business and systems analysis, system implementation, solution design, accounting, operational readiness and application support.

Her experience spans both business and technology functions, working closely with Front Office, Operations, Accounting, Application Support, Technology and software vendor teams. She has led implementation workstreams, supported testing and deployment, delivered user training, translated business requirements into system solutions, and guided clients through operational and technology change.

Before founding Emblaris, Mikaela spent 13 years with SimCorp in consulting, solutions engineering and leadership roles, most recently as Head of Solutions Engineering, North America, where she led a team of nine.
