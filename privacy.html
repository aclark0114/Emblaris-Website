Privacy policy

How Emblaris Inc. collects, uses and protects personal information.

Emblaris Inc. ("Emblaris", "we", "us") respects your privacy and handles personal information in accordance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and other applicable laws.

Information we collect

We collect personal information only when you choose to provide it, for example when you contact us by email, telephone or through our website. This may include your name, organization, email address, telephone number and the content of your message.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and communicate with you about our services
  • To manage our client relationships and deliver contracted services
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

Client confidentiality

Information our clients share with us in the course of an engagement is treated as confidential and is used only for the purposes of that engagement, in line with our contractual obligations.

Retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, and protect it with reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards.

Your rights

You may request access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent to its use, by contacting us at info@emblaris.ca.
