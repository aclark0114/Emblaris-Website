Services built for investment operations

We specialize in software and business transformation for financial institutions, helping you optimize your investment environment by improving processes, strengthening technology and aligning your operating model with evolving business needs.

Transformation strategy

Change in investment operations is rarely just a technology project. We help you set a clear direction that connects business objectives, operating realities and technology choices.

What this includes

  • Current-state assessment of processes, systems and data
  • Target-state vision aligned with long-term business objectives
  • Gap analysis and a prioritized, sequenced transformation roadmap
  • Platform and vendor evaluation support

The result: a shared, realistic plan your leadership, operations and technology teams can stand behind.

Operating model design

We design operating models that reflect how your business actually works, across Front Office, Operations, Accounting, Technology and your software vendors.

What this includes

  • Front-to-back process design and documentation
  • Roles, responsibilities and hand-offs between teams
  • IBOR and ABOR design and alignment
  • Controls, reconciliation and exception management

The result: clear ownership, fewer manual workarounds and an operation ready to scale.

Enterprise planning

Large programs succeed or stall on planning. We help you prioritize, sequence and resource change so it lands without disrupting the business.

What this includes

  • Program and workstream planning
  • Dependency, risk and issue management
  • Resource and capacity planning across business and technology
  • Stakeholder coordination and escalation frameworks

The result: a delivery plan that holds up when priorities shift.

Implementation advisory

We bring hands-on experience of leading implementation workstreams on enterprise investment platforms, bridging the gap between business teams, technology teams and software vendors.

What this includes

  • Business and systems analysis, and requirements definition
  • Solution design and configuration guidance
  • Test planning, execution support and deployment
  • User training and operational readiness
  • Post-go-live application support and stabilization

The result: a platform that works for the people who use it, delivered with fewer surprises.

Who we work with

Our clients are financial institutions with complex investment environments, primarily across North America.

Asset managers

Firms modernizing front-to-back platforms, consolidating systems or expanding into new asset classes.

Pension plans

Public and private plans managing multi-asset portfolios, including alternatives and OTC instruments.

Institutional investors

Organizations strengthening their investment accounting, data and reporting capabilities.

Whether you are selecting a platform, mid-implementation, or rethinking how your teams work, we are glad to talk it through.
